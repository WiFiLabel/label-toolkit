# Code of conduct

We want a useful library and a calm review queue.

- Critique the spec entry or the patch, and keep the person out of it.
- Assume a printer report is incomplete until the model, firmware, and `~HI` text are attached.
- Do not post credentials, customer labels, or shipping data in issues.
- Harassment, slurs, and repeated off-topic demands are not welcome. Maintainers may lock or close those threads.

Questions about how a command behaves belong in an issue with a `sourceRef` and, when you have it, a capture from a printer.
